SmartGrader Handwritten exams, graded end-to-end

Scanned handwritten exams go in; graded, rubric-scored results come out — with the teacher still in control of every mark.

  • periodsummer 2025
  • rolecapstone · eduhk hong kong
  • headline3-stage pipeline

the problem

Grading stacks of scanned handwritten exams is slow, repetitive, and error-prone — and handwriting recognition alone isn't grading. The system has to find the answers on the page, read them, judge them against a rubric, and still let the teacher overrule it.

Built as the capstone of an intensive deep-learning and object-detection summer at The Education University of Hong Kong — YOLO training, data annotation, and evaluation with mAP and CER.

how it works

  1. Locate — YOLOv8

    A trained YOLOv8 model detects and crops the answer sections on each scanned page, so downstream models read answers, not headers and doodles.

  2. Read — EasyOCR + TrOCR

    Two OCR engines team up on real student handwriting, converting each cropped region into text robust enough to score.

  3. Score — LLM against the rubric

    A language model evaluates each extracted answer against the teacher's rubric and proposes a mark with reasoning.

  4. Review — teacher in the loop

    A review/edit/export page shows every proposed score next to the original scan. The teacher confirms, adjusts, and exports — automation without surrendering judgment.
