Arabic Text Correction

A POS-aware AraT5 that catches and fixes Arabic grammatical errors — in a language where one wrong word can cascade through gender, number, and case.

  • rolesolo project
  • headline81.04 F0.5 on QALB-2014

the problem

Arabic grammatical error correction is significantly harder than English. Arabic is heavily inflected — verbs, nouns, and adjectives all change form with gender, number, case, and definiteness — so a correction isn't a substitution, it's a chain of morphological agreements. One error can cascade into several surface mistakes.

And the data is thin: QALB-2014 is the primary Arabic GEC benchmark, small by English standards. The model has to generalize from limited annotated examples.

how it works

  1. Prepare QALB-2014 for seq2seq

    Processed the benchmark — Arabic text with annotated errors and gold corrections — into clean input-output pairs for sequence-to-sequence training.

  2. Give the model morphology explicitly

    Applied Arabic POS tagging and appended the tags as auxiliary tokens, handing the model grammatical context that subword tokenization alone can't capture.

  3. Fine-tune AraT5

    AraT5-base brings strong Arabic priors from pre-training; task-specific fine-tuning sharpens it into a corrector.

  4. Score what users actually feel

    Evaluated with F0.5 — the standard GEC metric that punishes over-correction more than under-correction, because a tool that "fixes" correct text is worse than one that stays quiet.
