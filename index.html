6 AI models · 1 GPU

NeoBalady+ Multi-model AI for smart-city operations

Our graduation project: a multi-model AI backend exploring how a Balady-style municipal complaints workflow could run itself. A quantized vision-language model reads every photo complaint — classifies it, writes the description, catches day/night duplicates, sets objective priority over exaggerated reports, and verifies repairs from before/after shots. A Saudi-dialect Whisper handles voice notes. The whole academic prototype runs on a single GPU with live Gradio dashboards.

photo / voice complaint→ VLM · classify + describe→ dedupe + priority→ SLA forecast→ repair verified ✓
city street network→ ConvLSTM forecaster→ hotspot alerts before reports exist
citizen question⇄ RAG assistant
